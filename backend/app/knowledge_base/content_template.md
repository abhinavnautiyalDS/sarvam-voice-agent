## Institution overview

[Only approved facts: official name, location, accreditation, affiliations, official contact channels.]

## Programmes and courses

For each programme, record:
- Official programme name and award
- Duration and mode
- Campus/location
- Intake / application cycle
- Official programme page

## Eligibility and admissions

Record exact eligibility criteria, required qualifications, minimum marks, entrance tests, application steps, required documents, deadlines, and exceptions. Preserve conditions and qualifiers exactly.

## Fees and scholarships

Record the academic year, currency, fee components, payment schedule, refund rules, scholarship eligibility, award amount/range, renewal conditions, and source. Never combine values from different academic years.

## Placements and outcomes

Record cohort/year, denominator, metric definition, median/average distinction, exclusions, and official source. Do not convert highest package into typical outcomes.

## Campus, facilities, and student services

Record campus-specific availability and the date the information was verified.

## FAQs

Write one self-contained question and answer per item. Answers must be fully supported by a source passage and must not imply guarantees unless the source explicitly guarantees them.

## Content quality checklist

- [ ] Every factual claim has a traceable source.
- [ ] Time-sensitive values include an effective year/date.
- [ ] Programme, campus, and cohort names are unambiguous.
- [ ] No marketing claim is rewritten as a guaranteed outcome.
- [ ] Contradictions are resolved with the source owner before ingestion.
- [ ] Outdated documents are removed or clearly superseded.
- [ ] Sensitive personal information is excluded.

## Suggested chunking

Prefer one topic or one FAQ per chunk. Keep conditions, exceptions, dates, and the associated metric together in the same chunk. Include source/page/section metadata with every pre-chunked passage.
