# DV University — Admissions, Courses & Fees Handbook

**Reference cycle:** Academic Session 2026–27  
**Institution status:** Fictional demonstration university.

> IMPORTANT: DV University is a fictional institution created for the AURA product demonstration. It is inspired by the broad structure of a large public university in Delhi, but it is not Delhi University, is not affiliated with Delhi University, and its courses, fees, dates, policies and admission rules are illustrative. Never present this information as official Delhi University information.

## 1. University overview
DV University is a fictional multi-campus public university in Delhi, India. It offers undergraduate and postgraduate programmes through academic schools and constituent colleges. Its academic model includes classroom teaching, tutorials, practical laboratories, projects, internal assessment and end-semester examinations.

Academic schools:
- School of Arts and Humanities
- School of Commerce and Business
- School of Mathematical and Physical Sciences
- School of Computer and Information Sciences
- School of Social Sciences
- School of Life Sciences

This handbook describes regular, on-campus demonstration programmes. Do not promise online, distance, evening, hostel or international-student availability unless a separate verified notice is added.

### Undergraduate eligibility guidance
- Applicants must have passed Class 12 from a recognised board or equivalent.
- Subject prerequisites differ by programme. A science programme may require specified science subjects; B.Sc. Computer Science and B.Sc. Mathematics may require mathematics.
- Minimum marks, category relaxations, subject combinations and equivalence rules are programme- and admission-cycle-specific.
- Meeting minimum eligibility does not guarantee admission.
- Ask which programme the student is considering and which subjects they studied in Class 12 before giving programme-specific guidance.
- If the current subject rule or cutoff is not documented, say that the current admission bulletin must be checked. Never invent a cutoff or guarantee eligibility.

## 5. Demonstration admission process
This is a generic illustrative workflow for the fictional university. Actual process and dates must be replaced by an admission-cycle notice before any real enrolment use.

1. Explore programmes and check subject eligibility.
2. Read the current admission bulletin and important dates.
3. Create an applicant account on the fictional DV University admission portal.
4. Complete the application form with personal, academic and programme details.
5. Upload required documents in the specified format.
6. Pay the application fee and save the receipt/application number.
7. Follow the selection route: merit list, entrance assessment, interview or a combination, as specified for the programme.
8. If shortlisted, complete counselling and document verification within the stated deadline.
9. Accept the offered seat and pay the admission fee before the deadline.
10. Download the provisional admission confirmation and follow college reporting instructions.

**Do not invent a live application URL, application opening date, cutoff, merit list, seat availability or deadline.** If asked for current status not present in the knowledge base, explain that the latest official notice is required.

## 8. Counselling and student support
The admission counsellor can explain programme differences, typical subjects, eligibility checks, application steps, common documents and fee components. It can help a student prepare questions for the admissions office and summarise next steps after a programme is selected.

The counsellor must not:
- Claim to be a human university employee or official representative.
- Guarantee admission, scholarships, placement, a particular college or a cutoff.
- Claim that DV University is Delhi University or government-affiliated.
- Make up current notices, dates, seat counts, rankings, placement statistics or contact details.
- Give legal or financial guarantees.

## 9. Frequently asked questions
**Q: Is DV University the same as Delhi University?**  
A: No. DV University is a fictional demonstration institution used in this AURA project. It is not Delhi University and its illustrative information must not be treated as DU policy.

**Q: Can I get admission after Class 12?**  
A: Many undergraduate programmes in this demonstration catalogue accept Class 12 applicants, but subject prerequisites and selection routes vary. Tell me the course and your Class 12 subjects.

**Q: Is B.Sc. Computer Science open to students without mathematics?**  
A: The demonstration eligibility guidance expects mathematics for B.Sc. Computer Science. The exact rule must be verified in the current programme bulletin.

**Q: Is the fee paid every semester or every year?**  
A: The table shows illustrative annual totals. The actual payment schedule is not specified here and must be confirmed in the current fee notice.

**Q: Does meeting eligibility guarantee a seat?**  
A: No. Eligibility allows an application to be considered; selection depends on the published process, merit/entrance criteria and seat availability.

**Q: Are hostel charges included?**  
A: No. Hostel and mess charges are excluded from the illustrative annual totals.

**Q: What if my subject combination is different?**  
A: Share the programme name and your Class 12 subjects. I can explain what needs checking, but the current official eligibility notice is authoritative.

## 10. Information gaps and escalation
This demo handbook intentionally does not fabricate:
- Real admission portal URL and verified contact details.
- Current-year application opening/closing dates.
- Programme-wise official cutoffs and seat matrix.
- Official category-wise eligibility relaxations.
- Confirmed college-specific fee breakdowns.
- Current scholarship forms, award amounts and deadlines.
- Real-time application or merit-list status.

When asked about missing information, say: “I don't have a verified current notice for that detail. Please check the latest DV University admission bulletin or contact its admissions office.” Always make clear that the university is fictional.
